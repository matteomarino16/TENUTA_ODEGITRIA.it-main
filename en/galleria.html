Our Estate

Atmospheric shots of our venue and the surrounding nature

A glimpse of Tenuta Odegitria, the masseria with an 18th-century trulli hamlet in Martina Franca, in the heart of the Itria Valley: dry-stone trulli, the manor house, the 6×14 m swimming pool surrounded by greenery, the tennis court, the Italian garden and the oak grove where weddings are held.

Discover our six accommodations — Agrifoglio Room, Trullo dell’Aia, Trullo La Corte, Trullo Il Pagliaio, Il Palmento Suite and Pino e Lecci Apartment — or our weddings page.
