— YOUR STAY

Trullo suite for couples with a stone hot tub

Up to 2 guests (2 adults)

A trullo beautifully restored as a romantic suite, with an alcove bed and a stone hot tub.

Trullo Il Palmento Suite is a 30 m² trullo at Tenuta Odegitria, in Martina Franca in the Itria Valley, recently restored as a romantic suite. It is also the bridal suite for weddings held at the estate.

It features an entrance hall, a double bedroom with the bed set in an alcove under the trullo dome, a relaxation corner and a bathroom with shower and private stone hot tub (available for a supplement, bookable on site). Air conditioning and underfloor heating; fridge and coffee/tea maker.

A cot and high chair are available free of charge on request. The swimming pool, tennis court and shared garden are available to guests; a buffet breakfast is served in the breakfast room on request.

Ideal for: Couples, honeymoons, anniversaries and newlyweds on their wedding night.

— FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Trullo Il Palmento Suite

How many guests can Trullo Il Palmento Suite accommodate?

Up to 2 guests (2 adults). Beds: 1 double bed.

What type of accommodation is Trullo Il Palmento Suite?

A romantic trullo suite of 30 m² at Tenuta Odegitria, a masseria with an 18th-century trulli hamlet in Martina Franca (TA), in the Itria Valley, Puglia. Ideal for: couples, honeymoons, anniversaries and newlyweds on their wedding night.

Does Trullo Il Palmento Suite have a kitchen?

No, there is no kitchen (there is a fridge). A typical buffet breakfast is available on request for an extra charge.

Is the suite’s hot tub included in the price?

The private stone hot tub is not included in the room rate: it can be booked on site for the days you wish, for a daily supplement.

Is breakfast included?

A typical buffet breakfast, served in the breakfast room, is available on request for an extra charge, so you can also book room only.

Can guests use the swimming pool and tennis court?

Yes, guests have access to the estate’s outdoor swimming pool (6×14 m) and tennis court, shared with other guests. A shared hot tub is also available on request for an extra charge.

Is there parking and Wi‑Fi?

Yes: free on-site parking with an electric vehicle charging station, and free Wi‑Fi. A car is recommended to explore the Itria Valley.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome: please let us know when you book.

Can we stay with a baby?

Yes, a cot and high chair are available on request.

How do I book Trullo Il Palmento Suite and what are the conditions?

Online from this page, with real-time availability and prices, or by contacting us on +39 338 565 5351 (also WhatsApp) or at info@tenutaodegitria.it. A 30% deposit is required to confirm the booking; the balance is paid on arrival by credit card, bank transfer or cash. With the refundable rate, cancellation is free up to 30 days before arrival.

Where is Trullo Il Palmento Suite located?

At Tenuta Odegitria, Strada Cupa-Battaglini 32/A, 74015 Martina Franca (TA), Puglia, Italy: 2 km from the centre of Martina Franca, 4 km from Locorotondo and 1 km from the SS 172 “dei Trulli” road, in the heart of the Itria Valley.
