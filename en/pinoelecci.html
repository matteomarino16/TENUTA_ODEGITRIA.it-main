— YOUR STAY

B&B apartment for 4 guests with two bedrooms and a lounge

Up to 4 guests

Our largest accommodation: 50 m² on the first floor of the manor house, with an elegantly furnished lounge.

The Pino e Lecci Apartment is on the first floor of the manor house of Tenuta Odegitria, a recently restored masseria with trulli in Martina Franca, in the Itria Valley. At 50 m² it is the largest accommodation on the estate.

It has an elegantly furnished lounge, a double bedroom, a bedroom with two single beds and a large bathroom for the exclusive use of the apartment. It also has a balcony, fridge, TV, air conditioning in summer and heating in winter. There is no kitchen: it is designed for a bed & breakfast stay, with buffet breakfast on request.

Outside there is a private area with a garden table and chairs; the estate’s swimming pool and tennis court are available to guests.

Ideal for: Two couples of friends or families with older children.

— FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Pino e Lecci Apartment

How many guests can Pino e Lecci Apartment accommodate?

Up to 4 guests. Beds: 1 double bed + 2 single beds.

What type of accommodation is Pino e Lecci Apartment?

A B&B apartment in the masseria of 50 m² at Tenuta Odegitria, a masseria with an 18th-century trulli hamlet in Martina Franca (TA), in the Itria Valley, Puglia. Ideal for: two couples of friends or families with older children.

Does Pino e Lecci Apartment have a kitchen?

No, there is no kitchen (there is a fridge). A typical buffet breakfast is available on request for an extra charge.

Is Pino e Lecci suitable for two couples?

Yes: the two bedrooms (one double, one twin) are separate and share the lounge and a large bathroom for the exclusive use of the apartment.

Is breakfast included?

A typical buffet breakfast, served in the breakfast room, is available on request for an extra charge, so you can also book room only.

Can guests use the swimming pool and tennis court?

Yes, guests have access to the estate’s outdoor swimming pool (6×14 m) and tennis court, shared with other guests. A shared hot tub is also available on request for an extra charge.

Is there parking and Wi‑Fi?

Yes: free on-site parking with an electric vehicle charging station, and free Wi‑Fi. A car is recommended to explore the Itria Valley.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome: please let us know when you book.

Can we stay with a baby?

Yes, a cot and high chair are available on request.

How do I book Pino e Lecci Apartment and what are the conditions?

Online from this page, with real-time availability and prices, or by contacting us on +39 338 565 5351 (also WhatsApp) or at info@tenutaodegitria.it. A 30% deposit is required to confirm the booking; the balance is paid on arrival by credit card, bank transfer or cash. With the refundable rate, cancellation is free up to 30 days before arrival.

Where is Pino e Lecci Apartment located?

At Tenuta Odegitria, Strada Cupa-Battaglini 32/A, 74015 Martina Franca (TA), Puglia, Italy: 2 km from the centre of Martina Franca, 4 km from Locorotondo and 1 km from the SS 172 “dei Trulli” road, in the heart of the Itria Valley.
