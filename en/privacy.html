Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Data controller

Tenuta Odegitria SRLS, Strada Cupa-Battaglini 32/A, 74015 Martina Franca (TA), Italy. Email: info@tenutaodegitria.it – Tel. +39 338 565 5351.

What data we collect

This website does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools. We only process the data you voluntarily send us through the contact forms (name, phone, email, city, dates and message), by email, phone or WhatsApp. The forms do not store any data on the website: they open your email program with the message already filled in, which you decide whether to send.

Why we use it and legal basis

To answer your requests for information, quotes and bookings (pre-contractual measures and contract, art. 6.1.b GDPR) and to comply with legal obligations related to your stay, such as registering guests with the police authorities (art. 6.1.c GDPR).

Online bookings

Online bookings are handled by the Kross Booking platform, which processes data on our behalf under its own privacy policy, available on the booking system.

Third-party services

To display the website correctly, content is loaded from external providers: Google Maps, Google Fonts, Font Awesome icons (cdnjs/Cloudflare), the jQuery library (Microsoft) and the Kross Booking widget. These providers may receive your IP address and technical browsing data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep request data for as long as needed to handle them and, if you stay with us, for the periods required by tax and public security law.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing and data portability at any time (arts. 15-22 GDPR) by writing to info@tenutaodegitria.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).
