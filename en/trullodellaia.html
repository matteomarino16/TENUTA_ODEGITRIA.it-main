— YOUR STAY

Trullo for 4 guests with a stone fireplace and two entrances

Up to 4 guests (2 adults + 2 children/teens)

The most spacious of our trulli: 40 m² with a living room and a traditional stone fireplace.

Trullo dell’Aia is a 40 m² trullo apartment in the 18th-century trulli hamlet of Tenuta Odegitria, a masseria in Martina Franca, in the Itria Valley. It has two separate entrances.

It features a double bedroom with its own entrance door, a living room with a traditional stone fireplace and a sofa bed for two children or teens, an equipped kitchenette and a private bathroom. Air-conditioned in summer and heated in winter.

Outside there is a private area with a garden table and chairs; guests can use the estate’s shared swimming pool and tennis court.

Ideal for: Families and small groups looking for space and independence.

— FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Trullo dell’Aia

How many guests can Trullo dell’Aia accommodate?

Up to 4 guests (2 adults + 2 children/teens). Beds: 1 double bed + 1 double sofa bed.

What type of accommodation is Trullo dell’Aia?

A trullo apartment of 40 m² at Tenuta Odegitria, a masseria with an 18th-century trulli hamlet in Martina Franca (TA), in the Itria Valley, Puglia. Ideal for: families and small groups looking for space and independence.

Does Trullo dell’Aia have a kitchen?

Yes: equipped kitchenette with fridge.

Can the fireplace in Trullo dell’Aia be used?

The stone fireplace is a signature feature of the living room; please contact us in advance if you would like to use it during your stay.

Is breakfast included?

A typical buffet breakfast, served in the breakfast room, is available on request for an extra charge, so you can also book room only.

Can guests use the swimming pool and tennis court?

Yes, guests have access to the estate’s outdoor swimming pool (6×14 m) and tennis court, shared with other guests. A shared hot tub is also available on request for an extra charge.

Is there parking and Wi‑Fi?

Yes: free on-site parking with an electric vehicle charging station, and free Wi‑Fi. A car is recommended to explore the Itria Valley.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome: please let us know when you book.

Can we stay with a baby?

Yes, a cot and high chair are available on request.

How do I book Trullo dell’Aia and what are the conditions?

Online from this page, with real-time availability and prices, or by contacting us on +39 338 565 5351 (also WhatsApp) or at info@tenutaodegitria.it. A 30% deposit is required to confirm the booking; the balance is paid on arrival by credit card, bank transfer or cash. With the refundable rate, cancellation is free up to 30 days before arrival.

Where is Trullo dell’Aia located?

At Tenuta Odegitria, Strada Cupa-Battaglini 32/A, 74015 Martina Franca (TA), Puglia, Italy: 2 km from the centre of Martina Franca, 4 km from Locorotondo and 1 km from the SS 172 “dei Trulli” road, in the heart of the Itria Valley.
