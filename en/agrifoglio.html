— YOUR STAY

Family B&B room on the first floor of the manor house

Up to 4 guests (2 adults + 2 children/teens)

A bright room overlooking the trulli, on the first floor of the masseria’s historic manor house.

The Agrifoglio room is on the first floor of the manor house of Tenuta Odegitria, a recently restored historic masseria with trulli in Martina Franca, in the heart of the Itria Valley (Puglia). Two windows overlook the trulli.

It has a double bed and a double sofa bed, ideal for two children or teens, plus a private bathroom, fridge, TV, air conditioning and heating. There is no kitchenette: it is designed for a bed & breakfast stay, with a typical buffet breakfast available on request.

Guests have a private outdoor area and can use the estate’s shared swimming pool (6×14 m) and tennis court.

Ideal for: Couples and families with children who prefer a B&B stay.

— FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Agrifoglio Room

How many guests can Agrifoglio Room accommodate?

Up to 4 guests (2 adults + 2 children/teens). Beds: 1 double bed + 1 double sofa bed.

What type of accommodation is Agrifoglio Room?

A B&B room in the masseria of 20 m² at Tenuta Odegitria, a masseria with an 18th-century trulli hamlet in Martina Franca (TA), in the Itria Valley, Puglia. Ideal for: couples and families with children who prefer a B&B stay.

Does Agrifoglio Room have a kitchen?

No, there is no kitchen (there is a fridge). A typical buffet breakfast is available on request for an extra charge.

Is the Agrifoglio room suitable for a family?

Yes. Besides the double bed there is a double sofa bed for two children or teens, and a cot for babies aged 0–3 is available on request.

Is breakfast included?

A typical buffet breakfast, served in the breakfast room, is available on request for an extra charge, so you can also book room only.

Can guests use the swimming pool and tennis court?

Yes, guests have access to the estate’s outdoor swimming pool (6×14 m) and tennis court, shared with other guests. A shared hot tub is also available on request for an extra charge.

Is there parking and Wi‑Fi?

Yes: free on-site parking with an electric vehicle charging station, and free Wi‑Fi. A car is recommended to explore the Itria Valley.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome: please let us know when you book.

Can we stay with a baby?

Yes, a cot and high chair are available on request.

How do I book Agrifoglio Room and what are the conditions?

Online from this page, with real-time availability and prices, or by contacting us on +39 338 565 5351 (also WhatsApp) or at info@tenutaodegitria.it. A 30% deposit is required to confirm the booking; the balance is paid on arrival by credit card, bank transfer or cash. With the refundable rate, cancellation is free up to 30 days before arrival.

Where is Agrifoglio Room located?

At Tenuta Odegitria, Strada Cupa-Battaglini 32/A, 74015 Martina Franca (TA), Puglia, Italy: 2 km from the centre of Martina Franca, 4 km from Locorotondo and 1 km from the SS 172 “dei Trulli” road, in the heart of the Itria Valley.
