— TENUTA ODEGITRIA

An 18th-century masseria with trulli in the Itria Valley

Tenuta Odegitria was born from the owners’ wish to bring back to life the traditions of the past and a cultural heritage that reflects the simplicity and hard work of the local community.

The owners, Anna Maria Calella and Giovanni Conserva, live with their family in the central complex of trulli, “I Trulli del Massaro”, and are committed to offering guests a pleasant and peaceful stay, while respecting privacy, with B&B or holiday-home services among the scents and flavors of Puglia.

— FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Tenuta Odegitria

Where is Tenuta Odegitria?

At Strada Cupa-Battaglini 32/A, 74015 Martina Franca (TA), Puglia, Italy: 2 km from the centre of Martina Franca, 4 km from Locorotondo and 1 km from the SS 172 “dei Trulli” road, in the heart of the Itria Valley. The baroque old town of Martina Franca, Locorotondo, Alberobello, Cisternino and the other Itria Valley villages are a short drive away.

What kind of property is Tenuta Odegitria?

A masseria (traditional Apulian farmhouse estate) with an 18th-century trulli hamlet, recently restored and run directly by its owners, Anna Maria Calella and Giovanni Conserva, who live on the estate. It offers 6 accommodations as holiday rentals or bed & breakfast and hosts weddings and events.

What accommodations are available and for how many guests?

Six accommodations for 2 to 4 guests each, 21 beds in total: four trulli (Trullo dell’Aia, Trullo La Corte, Trullo Il Pagliaio and Trullo Il Palmento Suite) and two on the first floor of the manor house (Agrifoglio Room and Pino e Lecci Apartment). Trullo dell’Aia, La Corte and Il Pagliaio have a kitchenette.

What amenities are included?

A 6×14 m outdoor swimming pool, tennis court, free Wi‑Fi, free on-site parking, air conditioning, a garden with barbecue area and a private outdoor area for each accommodation. On request, for an extra charge: buffet breakfast and a shared hot tub. Cot and high chair on request. The car park has an electric vehicle charging station and pets are allowed.

What are the check-in and check-out times? Are pets allowed?

Check-in from 2:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am. Pets are welcome: please let us know when you book.

Is breakfast included?

A typical buffet breakfast is available on request for an extra charge, so you can also book room only.

How do I book and what are the conditions?

Directly on this website, with real-time availability and prices through the official booking system, or on +39 338 565 5351 (also WhatsApp) or at info@tenutaodegitria.it. A 30% deposit is required to confirm the booking; the balance is paid on arrival by credit card, bank transfer or cash. With the refundable rate, cancellation is free up to 30 days before arrival.

Can we hold a wedding or event?

Yes. The estate is an officially recognised civil registry office (art. 3 DPR 396/2000), so legally valid civil ceremonies are held on site. The Italian garden, oak grove, threshing floor, pool area, trulli hamlet and a hall with panoramic windows host events of up to about 200 guests, with free choice of caterer.

How far is Tenuta Odegitria from airports and nearby towns?

Approximate driving distances: Martina Franca old town 2 km, Locorotondo 4 km, Cisternino about 12 km, Alberobello about 13 km, Ostuni about 30 km, Taranto about 35 km, Adriatic Sea (Monopoli) about 35 km, Brindisi Airport about 60 km, Bari Airport about 80 km.

What is there to see nearby?

The Itria Valley: the baroque old town of Martina Franca (2 km), Locorotondo (4 km), the UNESCO-listed trulli of Alberobello, Cisternino and Ostuni, plus wineries, masserie and scenic roads among olive groves and vineyards. A car is recommended.

— EVENTS & WEDDINGS

Weddings and events at the estate

Tenuta Odegitria offers wide outdoor areas and alleys between the trulli, the garden around the pool, as well as a large wooden gazebo, for up to about 200 guests for events, exhibitions, conferences and weddings.

It is officially recognized as a separate Civil Registry Office for the celebration of weddings and civil unions pursuant to art. 3 of DPR 396/2000.

Nearby you will find the Church of Sant’Antonio ai Cappuccini, devoted to the Madonna dell’Odegitria, as well as the historic Baroque churches of Martina Franca for religious ceremonies. Lunch or dinner is provided by an external banqueting & catering service.
